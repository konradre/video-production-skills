# Contest hygiene — only when entering one

Read the rules snapshot AND the enforcement particulars ("find out the particulars of each"); the
ladder of consequences reaches WIP cuts, not just the final. What bit, in order:

- **Two clocks**: the race (a tie-break that rewards the earlier first-publication date, immutable)
  and the final deadline. Lock the premise BEFORE publishing WIP — continuity binds a published
  project to a good-faith continuation; a granted project cannot be unpublished.
- **Where generation must happen**: every AI video/image generation inside the one contest project on
  the platform, within the window. External POST is permitted (cut, grade, mask, titles, non-AI 3D);
  **external AI upscale, interpolation, relight and a NLE's neural tools are disqualifiers** — an
  external upscaler is not available; the reconstruction is the platform's own, one pass on the locked cut.
- **The audio asymmetry**: audio may be generated OFF-platform, but **every sound must be AI-generated**,
  ownership and licensing irrelevant — in the final AND in every published WIP cut; no temp track,
  ever; every file uploaded to the project; a file → tool → version → date manifest.
- **Likeness**: no identifiable real person's likeness or voice, including your own and historical
  figures, with no consent exception; characters who read as under 18 are prohibited.
- **Content**: no political or religious STATEMENT (a mythic structure is fine; a statement is not);
  no third-party IP, brands or logos.
- **Retention**: generation history, prompts, project files and audio provenance archived for the
  audit period (24 months); the final cut must reconcile against the project's own generation history.
- **Submission**: runtime floor, aspect, container, subtitles or VO, watermark and packshot on the film
  and the post, a public post from a public account viewable logged out, the roster locked, metadata
  and tags complete, before the deadline in the rules' time zone.
- **Ask what the rules do not say** (how many entries per person, whether a grey-zone tool counts as
  generation) in-product before spending.

A production may pivot away from a contest; the method above survives as a checklist and carries no
project state.
