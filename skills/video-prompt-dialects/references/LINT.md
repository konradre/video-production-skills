# Lint rules — each from an incident

`scripts/prompt_lint.py` implements the mechanical rows; the judgement rows are read by eye before the
gate. FAIL blocks; WARN is answered in the prompt or in the GO ask.

| # | check | level | the incident |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Slot ranges**: expand every `@refN–@refM` / `@ImageN`; no index assigned two meanings; no gap below the maximum; maximum ≤ the venue cap | FAIL | two of twelve prompts closed a range at the prompt's LAST slot instead of the group's (`@ref4–@ref9 the three creatures`), silently giving one slot two meanings; nothing complained, it rendered slightly wrong and billed. The linter itself needed a regression set: three versions cried wolf on correct prompts |
| L2 | **Char cap** per venue (2000 front-end; 6000 wire; warn > 5000) — re-measured after EVERY edit | FAIL | an edit for another reason pushed two prompts back over the cap |
| L3 | **Negation in the body** (`no / never / not / does not / nothing … ` outside the final section or tail list) | WARN | `no head bounce` → a bounce; `THEY DO NOT MOVE IN UNISON` → unison; `Nothing lumbers` rode in every lumbering take. Sanctioned tail classes on Seedance: subtitles, music, logos, watermarks + the shot document's own exclusions |
| L4 | **ALL-CAPS tokens** outside the stoplist (sound words, role names the gate rules) | WARN | ALL-CAPS script quotes trip the refs gate's subject scan; quote lines in sentence case |
| L5 | **Moderation words** for the venue (Seedance: the anatomical nouns, "thrusting"; injury words in slapstick: hit/strike/crush/knocked out) | WARN | "penis" + "thrusting" → `nsfw` on all three seeds; slapstick passed with "sweeps him clean off his feet" |
| L6 | **Parameters in the prose** (resolution tokens, "9:16" outside the prefix, "N seconds long", model names) | WARN | the vendor's rule; a `FORMAT` line once carried all five |
| L7 | **Off-frame object named** ("below the frame", "out of frame", "off-screen" + a noun) | WARN | "his bag on the carpet (below the frame)" ×3 → the camera pulled back and invented a handbag (20 cr) |
| L8 | **Category referent / gait mechanics** ("like big wild animals"; heel strike, hip extension, knee lock, toe-off; > 2 locomotion terms) | WARN | `big wild animals` → the wrong animal on all fours; six gait constraints → sideways hopping; two terms → coherent |
| L9 | **"aerial view"** | WARN | failed three times; "a wide landscape photograph taken from a high ridge top" passed |
| L10 | **Camera move + diverging subjects** (push/dolly in + "run apart / diverge / scatter") | WARN | the model widened to keep three separating creatures in frame and ignored the push |
| L11 | **Framing not on its own clause** (a cut without a shot-size token before its first action verb) | WARN | "medium shot from the waist up" inside the event sentence came out wider than the same words on their own line |
| L12 | **Beat density** > 1 beat per 3 s in a cut (count of action verbs / seconds) | WARN | five beats in 13 s rushed every one (3.28 Mbps); three beats of one action = the keeper (5.27 Mbps) |
| L13 | **A reference described** (an appearance adjective in the same clause as `@ImageN` beyond a role verb) | WARN | one wrong colour word beside the correct sheet on every generation — the words won |
| L14 | **Start state contradicts the previous keeper** ("a few last pieces drift down" after a burst; "seated" after a stand-up) | eye | a fresh bang at frame 1; a character seated again |
| L15 | **Line-deliverer's addressee missing** (a quoted line with no "toward X" / "at the camera" target) | eye | the speaker turned to the lens instead of the addressee |
| L16 | **Cast not closed** ("guests", "crowd", "partygoers", "everyone else", "packed" without "only these N") | WARN | invented women and men on two seeds |
| L17 | **The first word of each line** — whisper the landed takes; a layout change is A/B'd same-seed before adoption | post | "Okay." dropped 2/2 under one layout, kept 2/2 under the other |
| L18 | **Self-contradiction with the template tail** (a body clause asking for text/labels while the tail forbids them) | WARN | "a printed paper label" + "No text, captions, labels" → a blurred smear |
| L19 | **Negatives audited against the references** (a boilerplate negative that contradicts what the reference depicts) | eye | "not a costume, not a person in a suit" fought practical-effects reference photos and had to be removed |
| L20 | **Physics chain** — name every moving thing, its position and heading; walk the chain | eye | a rock thrown from behind a receding truck cannot reach the side window; three more contradictions found by the walk |
| L21 | **Sightline before a facing** — where is the camera, where did the subject come from, where are they going | eye | "back half to camera" for a man walking toward the camera → face out of frame AND identity lost |
| L22 | **Reveal assets absent from earlier shots**; **story-level foreclosure** (what a shot gives away) | eye | a reveal asset visible before the pan that reveals it; a scale prop gave the creature away in shot 1 |
| L23 | **A look plate restated in prose** (a cited look plate AND a grade sentence describing the same light — the late-asset stand-in) | eye | ten of twelve prompts, written before the plates existed, paid characters twice; reclaim only true re-description, only under a tight cap |
| L24 | **Every citation resolves to an asset; every asset is cited** (a set-difference both ways against the job document) | eye | four cited plates never existed as assets; a camp plate and three superseded specs sat orphaned |
| L25 | **One camera clause for a multi-cut clip** (a single move described while the event script cuts between cameras) | eye | one camera basis cannot describe an edit; a sentence claiming one move beside a reference video that cuts contradicts it (the compiler's multi-shot gate, 09-10) |
| L26 | **A proxy render cited without its role** (`@VideoN` of a grey-box clip with no motion-only clause, or `@ImageN` of a grey still with no layout-only clause) | eye | a labelled schematic rendered its labels into 2/3 takes; the role lines held on 09-10 — no grey leaked from a clip or a still — mandatory; a proxy rides only with every fixed-set object modelled, or the take learns the absence |
| L27 | **An H3 shot shipped without its proxy clip — a static shot included** (a scene proxy exists for the shot, the prompt carries a camera clause, and no `<Video N>` cites the clip) | eye | the clause alone re-staged the room from behind a sofa absent from the reference; with `<Video 1>` the camera ran the authored arc and the room held; a static clip held a static shot at 54–66 dB where prose alone had invented a push-in (09-10) |
| L28 | **Beat density on an H3 shot** (more than three expression beats — brow, eye, mouth events — inside one `[Shot N]` or one timecode span) | eye | nine beats in one 7 s close-up rendered a still face at 37–42 dB; split into 2–3 s shots with one beat each, every beat landed (`h3-storyboard-skill`, controlled, 08-26) |
| L29 | **A stillness instruction spanning a shot** ("nothing changes", "stays where it is", "does not move" written as the shot's state rather than one object's) | eye | the instruction leaked across the whole shot and froze the beats written beside it (08-26, not isolated from L28); the pause is an edit |
| L30 | **A fraction or a unit as a size or spacing instruction** ("two thirds as tall as the frame", "a hand's width short of", "five centimetres away") | eye | fractions rendered 45–52 % for a 67 % target; the unit distance was ignored and the liquid reached the base; a crop relationship and an in-frame ruler rendered 69 % (08-25/26) |

## The regression discipline for the linter itself

A check ships only at zero false positives on the known-good prompt set, and every loosening is replayed
against the original defects (the two range-close errors) to prove it still fires — otherwise "PASS" only
means the check got quieter.
| L31 | **A clause naming an input the call does not carry** (`<Video N>` cited with no video ref passed, or the proxy-clip role line present with no `<Video N>` cited) | FAIL | H3 has ONE positive stream and no negative prompt, so "do not reproduce its proxy geometry, grid, markers" with the clip dropped is an instruction to DRAW them: a floor/ceiling grid plus cyan and red markers on every take, escalating to full cyan wireframes over the cast on the Comfy-Org base trunk. Deleting the clause → clean (still-lock 0.995); restoring a small clip so the clause has its referent → also clean, and the proxy's own grid correctly suppressed. **Keep both or remove both** (local H3, 2026-09-11) |
| L32 | **A speech tag outside the dialogue envelope** (`<i>`, `<laughs>`, `<sighs>` … not inside a `<d>…</d>` span) | WARN | a bare tag is SPOKEN — the model read the bracket aloud as "br" and "le-incredible" on some seeds; the full trained envelope `(S1) says: <d>[English] We are <i>going</i> to do <i>incredible</i> things!</d>` stopped it, replicated across seeds. Residual: with two `<i>` spans only the first was emphasised (community-tested, 2026-09-11) |
| L33 | **Beauty-adjective phrasing in an identity description** (generic attractiveness language — `attractive`, `beautiful`, `stunning`, `soft oval face`, `straight slender nose`, `flawless skin` — in a prompt that also cites an identity reference) | WARN | reported to activate the model's generalised beauty prior and overwrite the face the reference carries: the citation still reads correct while the take drifts toward a generic pretty face. Write biometric traits — the actual nose, jaw, eye colour and spacing, hairline, asymmetries, marks, age in the face. **Reported by an upstream guide, not measured here** (2026-09-11) — settle it with a same-seed A/B, biometric vs beauty phrasing against one identity still, faces read at 1:1 |
| L34 | **Single-shot negatives on a long or multi-cut generation** ("no unmotivated cut / hidden splice / abrupt push-in / jump cut" with more than one `Cut N`, or a span over 6 s from the cut budgets or `--duration`) | WARN | a 20 s refs-only generation carried the single-shot tail inherited from 4–6 s prompts, forbidding the coverage the long take existed to compose — a cut inside one generation is descriptive, consistency across it the verdict (2026-09-13) |
| L35 | **A quiet H3 shot's soundscape written as an absence** ("quiet", "silence", "no speech", "nobody speaks" in `overall_soundscape`, no sound source named) | eye | "the room's natural quiet; no speech, no music" left invented sound in 2 of 6 local takes; a named room tone with `non_diegetic_music: N/A` left it in 0 of 6 and a calmer mouth (2026-09-19). H3 fills an empty audio lane with talk: name what the room sounds like |
